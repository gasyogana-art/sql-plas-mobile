`wire`: Used for combinational logic and signal connections, cannot store values, driven by `assign` statements.
`reg`: Used for sequential logic, can store values, assigned within always blocks.

Reminders:
・Variables assigned within an always block must be of type reg.
・If a signal is not explicitly declared as wire or reg, it defaults to wire.
